You are always welcome here

Our doors are open to everyone. Together we get to know God, grow in faith and support one another.

First time here?

Answers to questions before your first visit.

No registration needed. Simply come along.

The service usually lasts about 90 minutes.

What can I expect?

You can expect worship, prayer, a clear message and community with people from different generations and cultures.

How do German and Russian work in the service?

The service is held in German and Russian. Content is translated or presented alternately in both languages.

Can I bring my children?

Our Sunday school takes place alongside the service at 10:00. Contact us beforehand to find out which age groups can currently take part.

Where can I park?

Parking is available near our church. It is best to allow a few extra minutes to find a space.

Is the entrance wheelchair accessible?

Yes. Our church has step-free access and there is a lift in the building.

Can I come alone or for the first time?

Yes. Come on your own, with friends or with your family. You are welcome even if you have never been to church before.

What should I wear?

There is no special dress code. Come in whatever makes you feel comfortable.

Do you have a question before your visit? Feel free to contact us.

What we believe

Our faith is grounded in the Bible and in Jesus Christ. As a Pentecostal free church, we believe the Holy Spirit is still at work today.

God

We believe in one God, the creator of heaven and earth, who reveals himself as Father, Son and Holy Spirit.

The Bible

The Bible is God’s word and the trustworthy foundation for our faith and our lives.

Jesus Christ

Jesus Christ is the Son of God. Through his death and resurrection, he offers forgiveness and new life.

Salvation by grace

Salvation is a gift from God. Through faith in Jesus Christ, people receive forgiveness and are reconciled with God.

The Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit is at work today, changing people and empowering them to live their faith and serve others.

Prayer

In prayer we speak with God, listen to him and trust that he meets us and works in our lives.

Baptism

Those who believe in Jesus Christ may choose to be baptised and publicly profess their faith.

Communion

In communion we remember the death and resurrection of Jesus and celebrate fellowship with him and with one another.

The church

The church is a community where people encounter God, grow in faith and care for one another.

Our hope

We believe that Jesus Christ will return and that God gives eternal life to all who trust in him.

We are a Pentecostal church within the Association of Pentecostal Churches in Germany (BFP).

Pastors

Vjacheslav and Oxana Schilling are the pastors of New Life Christian Church in Plauen.

Together they lead and care for the church. Vjacheslav is its founder and lead pastor.
Vjacheslav Schilling is the founder and pastor of the Christian Church "New Life" in the city of Plauen.

Vjacheslav was born in Almaty, Kazakhstan. After arriving in Germany with his family, he founded a Russian-speaking Church "New Life" in 2005.

Pastor Vjacheslav is a preacher and speaker at many conferences and seminars.

Vjacheslav Schilling lives with his family in the city of Plauen and has two children.
